How the AI Model Was Built

From public data to a tested, laptop-ready model · SIF-Precursor Engine · SIH 26165

What went in

  • • 163k reports in total
  • • All public (OSHA, NASA) or AI-written
  • • No company's private data used
  • • No real worker's report was needed

The anti-cheating trick

  • • Words that reveal the outcome were hidden
  • • The model can't shortcut to the answer
  • • It must learn from the situation itself
  • • That's what makes it honest on new reports

How we know it works

  • • 500 reports labeled blind by humans
  • • Precision 0.98 — flags are almost always right
  • • Recall 0.84 — catches most real dangers
  • • A safety officer still has the final say
